Our Data

Our data includes speeches and public appearances from the Ministries of Foreign Affairs of the People's Republic of China and Russian Federation. Our dataset spans from 2005 to 2025, depending on the country, and provides coverage of major and minor diplomatic statements and addresses. For a more detailed description of our data visit our data dictionary. For the code we used to scrape the data check out our gitbub repositories for the China and Russia scrapers.

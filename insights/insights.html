Vizualizations

About This Tool

  • This tool visualizes trends in diplomatic rhetoric over time using more than 4,000 official speeches from the Chinese and Russian Ministries of Foreign Affairs.
  • Keywords of geopolitical relevance are pre-tagged; custom searches are available on our data page.
  • The charts show the number of speeches per year containing selected keywords for each country, with an option to view results as a share of total speeches in a given year.
  • This allows users to compare how China and Russia prioritize and discuss key geopolitical issues over time.

    • Example Explorations

    Comparative Analysis

    Relative Speeches Containing ASEAN Over Time

    Russian references to ASEAN have caught up with China's in recent years. This reflects Moscow's growing interest in Southeast Asia after elevating its relationship with ASEAN to a strategic partnership in 2018.

    Per-Country Analysis

    Chinese speeches containing "reunification" with and without "peaceful".

    Though historically closely correlated, mentions of "peaceful reunification" have declined in recent years compared to "reunification" alone. This reflect shifts in Beijing's rhetoric towards Taiwan.

    Term Frequency-Inverse Document Frequency

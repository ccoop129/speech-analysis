Overview

Curated insights and tools to generate your own

Official speeches are a primary way governments signal priorities, justify actions, and shape international narratives. This platform systematically analyzes rhetoric over time, transforming thousands of speeches into structured data and interactive visualizations.

By examining keyword frequency, entities, and trends in context, users can identify how China and Russia prioritize and discuss key geopolitical issues in response to global events—at a scale not possible through manual reading alone.

Example

One pattern, surfaced at scale

After the full-scale invasion of Ukraine, BRICS featured significantly more prominently in the speeches of Russian Foreign Minister Sergei Lavrov. By 2024, roughly 30 percent of his speeches and public appearances featured references to BRICS, potentially reflecting Russia’s priority of promoting alternatives to Western-led institutions and aligning itself with other emerging powers representative of the “global majority” in an emerging multipolar international system.

Why it matters

Why analyzing official rhetoric matters

Important diplomatic signals are often embedded in foreign languages, lengthy speeches, and fragmented official sources. By focusing on primary source material—official government speeches—this project reduces the time and language barriers involved in understanding how leaders frame policy issues over time and across regions.

Rather than offering editorial interpretation, the platform enables users to conduct their own analysis, emphasizing patterns, trends, and language usage to support independent, evidence-based conclusions.
